# MatVerse Canonical Taxonomy

This document defines the cleaned canonical ordering for the MatVerse ecosystem before the final institutional landing page is authored.

## 1. Canonical order

```text
MatVerse → ACOA → Symbios → Cassandra → Atlas → Gate
```

This order is intentional. It separates institution, architecture, systems, persona/interface, constitution and execution.

## 2. Primary entities

### MatVerse

**Role:** institute / institutional field.

MatVerse is the scientific, institutional and constitutional umbrella. It is not a single app, product, dashboard or chatbot. It is the institute-level frame that organizes research, systems, governance, products, evidence and public positioning.

Public description:

> MatVerse is the institute for verifiable intelligence and complex systems governance.

### ACOA

**Role:** architectures.

ACOA is the architecture layer. It defines structural organization, autopoiesis, antifragility, governance patterns, adaptive loops and system coherence.

Public description:

> ACOA designs the architectures that allow MatVerse systems to evolve without losing coherence.

### Symbios

**Role:** systems.

Symbios is the system and product layer. It includes OS, OSX, apps, dashboards, operational tools, mobile surfaces, automations and user-facing systems.

Public description:

> Symbios turns MatVerse architecture into operational systems and usable experiences.

### Cassandra

**Role:** multimodal spokesperson, model, persona and cognitive interface.

Cassandra is the bridge between humans, architectures and systems. It is the voice, multimodal persona, semantic router, organizer and operational communicator of the ecosystem. Functionally, Cassandra is the MatVerse J.A.R.V.I.S.

Public description:

> Cassandra is the multimodal spokesperson and cognitive interface of MatVerse.

### Atlas

**Role:** constitution.

Atlas is the constitutional layer. It defines laws, invariants, boundaries, taxonomies, admissibility criteria and canonical structure.

Public description:

> Atlas is the constitution of MatVerse: laws, invariants and boundaries.

### Gate

**Role:** executor.

Gate is the execution and enforcement layer. It applies Atlas, receives routed intent and context from Cassandra, enforces admissibility, and drives execution through governed mechanisms.

Public description:

> Gate executes under constitutional constraints.

## 3. Everything else

Everything outside the six primary entities is classified as one of the following:

```text
skills
agents
algorithms
engines
products
labs
trails
research modules
proof mechanisms
```

These elements may be important, but they are not primary sovereign entities.

## 5. Public landing rule

The final canonical landing must not present every internal invention as a primary entity.

The public hierarchy should be:

```text
MatVerse as institute
ACOA as architectures
Symbios as systems
Cassandra as multimodal spokesperson / J.A.R.V.I.S.
Atlas as constitution
Gate as executor
```

Then, below that, explain that all remaining capabilities are implemented as skills, agents and algorithms.
